Start Your Healthcare Career

Industry-Recognized Healthcare Skill Programmes

Government-recognized, NSQF-aligned healthcare skill programmes designed to develop practical competencies, professional confidence, and career readiness for the healthcare workforce.

A Career Opportunity for Every Aspirant

Not every student follows the traditional path to higher education. The Healthcare Sector Skill Council (HSSC) has developed these nationally recognized skill programmes to create meaningful employment opportunities for school leavers and young adults aspiring to work in healthcare.

Aligned with the National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF), these programmes combine classroom learning, practical training and workplace exposure to prepare learners for entry-level healthcare careers.

Successful candidates become eligible for employment opportunities in hospitals, diagnostic centres and other healthcare organizations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join these programmes?

Students who have completed the eligibility criteria specified for the respective job role and are interested in building a career in healthcare.

Are these courses government recognised?

Yes. The programmes are aligned with the National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF) and follow Healthcare Sector Skill Council (HSSC) job role standards.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible candidates who successfully complete training and assessment receive the applicable certification.

Does RHSA provide placement support?

RHSA provides placement guidance and connects eligible learners with employment opportunities whenever available.

How do I choose the right course?

Our counsellors help students understand the different job roles and select the programme best suited to their interests and career goals.

How can I apply?

You can submit an enquiry through the website, visit our training centre or contact our admissions team.
