Overview

The Medical Records Assistant (MRA) programme prepares learners to manage patient health records accurately, securely and efficiently in modern healthcare organizations. Students develop practical skills in medical records management, hospital information systems, medical terminology, coding fundamentals and healthcare documentation.

Developed in accordance with the Healthcare Sector Skill Council (HSSC) Model Curriculum, the programme combines classroom learning, practical skill development and workplace exposure to prepare learners for rewarding careers in healthcare information management.

Eligibility

  • 10th Pass + One year completed of any 2/3-year Diploma programme.
  • OR 12th Pass (Science Stream) – Fresher.
  • OR 10th Pass with one year of relevant hospital experience.
  • Basic computer knowledge is desirable.

Learning Outcomes

  • Maintain and organize patient medical records accurately.
  • Understand Hospital Information Management Systems (HIMS).
  • Apply medical terminology in healthcare documentation.
  • Perform basic medical coding and documentation activities.
  • Ensure confidentiality and legal compliance while handling patient information.
  • Support healthcare teams through efficient records management and documentation.
