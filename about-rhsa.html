WHO WE ARE

Empowering People. Elevating Healthcare.

Resilient Healthcare Skill Academy (RHSA) is the learning wing of M&M Resilient Ventures Pvt. Ltd., committed to developing a skilled, ethical and future-ready healthcare workforce through practical education, industry collaboration and lifelong learning.

From entry-level healthcare skill development to professional development programmes for hospitals and health & wellness education for schools, RHSA builds capability across every stage of the healthcare ecosystem.

We work with students, healthcare professionals, hospitals, educational institutions and industry partners to create meaningful learning experiences that improve careers, strengthen organizations and ultimately contribute to better health outcomes.
