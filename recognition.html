Industry Recognition

Resilient Healthcare Skill Academy (RHSA), the learning vertical of M&M Resilient Ventures Pvt. Ltd., is committed to delivering quality healthcare skill development programmes aligned with nationally recognized standards. Our recognitions reflect our commitment to structured training, assessment readiness, and workforce development.

Health Records & Information Assistant (MRA)

RHSA is recognized by the Health Sector Skill Council (HSSC) as a Training Centre for the role of Health Records & Information Assistant (MRA).

Front Desk Coordinator (FDC)
