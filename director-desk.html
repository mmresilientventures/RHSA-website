A Message from the Director

A personal note to every learner beginning their journey with RHSA.

Dear Learner,

Welcome to Resilient Healthcare Skill Academy. Healthcare is more than a profession—it is a commitment to serve with knowledge, compassion and integrity. Every competent healthcare professional has the power to improve lives, strengthen communities and make a meaningful difference where it matters most.

RHSA was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to bridge the gap between education and employability by providing practical, industry-relevant and competency-based healthcare training. Our programmes are designed not only to prepare you for employment but also to help you build confidence, professionalism and a lifelong commitment to excellence.

As you begin your journey with RHSA, remember that skills create opportunities, values build trust and continuous learning shapes leaders. We are committed to supporting you at every step and helping you grow into a capable healthcare professional who can make a positive impact on patients, organizations and society.

I welcome you to the RHSA family and wish you every success in your journey ahead.

Warm Regards,

Dr. (Major) Meeta Tyaagi

About Me

A journey of service, leadership, learning and continuous transformation.

I am a physician, healthcare educator, healthcare administrator, entrepreneur and author with over three decades of experience across clinical practice, hospital administration, healthcare quality, medical education and skill development.

My professional journey has taken me from serving in the Indian Army Medical Corps and AIIMS New Delhi to establishing M&M Resilient Ventures Pvt. Ltd. and the Resilient Healthcare Skill Academy (RHSA). Each phase has strengthened my belief that quality healthcare begins with competent, compassionate and confident professionals.

Through RHSA, my mission is to bridge the gap between education and employability by developing practical healthcare skills, strengthening healthcare systems and nurturing future-ready professionals who can positively impact patient care and society.
