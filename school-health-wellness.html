What Students Learn

A comprehensive curriculum that equips students with practical life skills, health awareness, safety practices and emergency preparedness.

Basic Health & Hygiene

Develop healthy habits and understand the importance of personal hygiene for lifelong wellbeing.

First Aid & Emergency Care

Learn to respond confidently during medical emergencies until professional help arrives.

Personal Safety & Awareness

Build awareness about personal safety, responsible behaviour and everyday risk prevention.

Digital Safety & Responsibility

Promote safe online practices, responsible technology use and cyber awareness.

Mental Health & Wellbeing

Encourage emotional resilience, stress management and positive mental wellbeing.

Healthy Lifestyle & Nutrition

Promote balanced nutrition, physical activity and healthy lifestyle choices.

Environment & Sustainability

Foster environmental responsibility and sustainable practices within school and community.

Disaster Preparedness

Build preparedness for natural and man-made emergencies through practical response skills.
